description = Text chat for multiplayer in any level or game mode. Press Enter to talk and pick how you are heard right on the input line (Tab or click): Speak - a speech bubble over your head and a babble in your own cartoon voice for players within 20 m (WRITE IN CAPS to shout farther); Whisper - a pale lavender bubble and a breathy babble, only within 8 m; Global - a plain chat line for the whole server (no bubble, no babble). The chat window keeps everything you heard; its Settings button lets you pick your voice. Commands: /s, /w, /g, /voice, /settings, /hint, /help. Every language supported. In multiplayer the host enables it for the session (Global mods); joining players get it from the Workshop.
